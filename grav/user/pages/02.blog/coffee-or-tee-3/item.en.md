===

Let's get straight to what and how. So, let us explain how to extract fragrant and delicious tea.
1. Find the tea with the aroma and taste you like
2. Find a sealed container that does not spoil the taste or smell
3. Brown silicate glass container or heat resistant glass container

You can see it all in the first picture. How to access?
1. Boil water
2. Pour water into the pot and pour 1-2 spoons of dry tea into the filter in the middle of the pot.
3. The container below has a metal shelf inside and a tea candle slot in the middle, where you can light the candle and put the tea on it (can be placed on the stove or stove on a low flame).
4. After 8 minutes, take out the filter with the tea inside and enjoy the tea

When making tea using the above method, all the flavors of the tea are absorbed into the water and the possibility of bitterness is reduced.
