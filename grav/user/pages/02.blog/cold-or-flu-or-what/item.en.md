It's a cold and flu. I just suddenly thought about it, the reason is because I'm lying on the couch in front of the TV with the flu right now, 😅😂 ... I didn't laugh a lot because I had a fever for 2 days, but I'm interested in making some things a habit and overcoming them easily next time.

===

![erkaeltungen_980x450px-mongol](erkaeltungen_980x450px-mongol.jpg "erkaeltungen_980x450px-mongol")

At first, I thought I had a cold, so I treated myself for 2 days, but on the 3rd day, the fever increased, so I went to the doctor yesterday: - Oh, you have the flu. And the medicine given is Ibuprofen 600 mg, to be taken 3 times a day after meals and 3 liters of water a day. Actually, yesterday I took 3 pills and got up today... However, in the evening of the 3rd day, the symptoms suddenly appeared and I was immediately put to bed, so it was tiring. Especially since it is interesting to overcome the cold by using less medicine. If I had a cold, I almost recovered within 2 days 🤣🤣, because I have learned to take care of myself, 😎. Foreigners don't pay much attention to colds, but stay away from flu. But who likes a person who makes fun of tears, 😜.

!! Attention! I'm not a doctor!
!! Always ask your doctor first! Thank you, 🤗.
!! Plus, everyone has their own unique physiology, so every treatment should be unique!
!! Illustrations are for general guidance and easy understanding only!

Cold or flu? The question was based on this. And when they say that they have the flu, they say it's their spouses, 🤧🤓. As for me, why didn't I go to the doctor for 2 days, the first symptoms were like a common cold, so I was deceived by the fact that I had a fever, but it was mild. Although the picture shows that the cold lasts longer, the first 2 days of the symptoms are a little painful for me, but the flu symptoms can be very painful at the same time, and it can sometimes last up to 21 days.

!!! ! I have bad habits: I've learned to ignore most things from sleep, fatigue, and pain, so I almost always miss or miscalculate the symptoms of illness, 😜🤪. To sweat against fatigue, to rest against illness, to eat well, to go out in the wind... the mountain has an easy principle, 🙂🙃.
