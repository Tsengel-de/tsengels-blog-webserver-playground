!!! So, without further ado, let me tell you about the research that has been done around the world!

1. According to a study conducted by Hoorn in 2002, the consumption of **coffee** has the effect of protecting and preventing type 2 diabetes. An analysis of more than 17,000 healthy people over many years showed that those who drank seven cups of coffee a day had a halved risk of developing type 2 diabetes. In addition, an inverse relationship between the amount of coffee consumed and the risk of type 2 diabetes can be shown. Additional studies from Sweden, Finland, Greece, Japan, and the United States came to the same conclusion: the higher the coffee intake, the lower the risk of type 2 diabetes.

2. **Coffee and Cardiovascular Disease**: Findings from a Multi-Country Study[энд](https://www.doctors.today/kinder/a/kaffee-risiko-oder-schutzfaktor-1574670)According to the report, it has been confirmed that it does not have any adverse effects on the cardiovascular system. On the contrary, the **risk** of people who drink **3-4 cups of coffee** a day** has been written as **-13% for men and -18% for women** in 10 years.

3. **No increased risk of stroke**: A 24-year trial of 86,000 people found no adverse effects. However, a person who does not drink at all suddenly consumes a lot and it is a problem, and at first it feels like heart palpitations and it lasts for about 7 days. I don't remember what happened to me, when I was a student, I drank too much until the night I was preparing for an exam, 🤣. Now I drink a double espresso even before going to bed. I advise people who are just starting to use **it is better to start with 1 cup a day and get used to it**. Also, if possible, don't drink after 3 p.m. Anyway, until your body gets used to the effects of coffee...

4. **How ​​does coffee affect blood pressure?**: According to research, people who are just starting to use it, it changes only in the first week, but only in the first 7 days... if you use it regularly, it has nothing to do with it.

5. **Coffee and cancer**: It has also been proven that the chances of getting cancer are lower or at least not negatively affected for many types of cancer.

6. **Coffee has a positive effect on the liver**: It has been known for years that coffee consumption has a positive effect on the liver. As early as 1986, a Scandinavian study identified a negative correlation between gamma-GT values ​​and coffee consumption. Additional studies in Italy and Asia confirmed these results. Japanese and Koreans from Asia have done a lot of research in this area. Also, coffee consumption reduces the risk of cirrhosis.

7. **Coffee and senile dementia (dementia)**: A study of 22,658 people over a 30-year period showed a 60% reduction in the risk of dementia and Alzheimer's disease with 3-5 cups of coffee per day.

8. **Coffee and Death**: According to the analysis of many years of research, if coffee is consumed correctly, it is estimated that it increases the life expectancy and reduces the total number of deaths.

### Conclusion

Scientific research over the past few years has shown that drinking coffee can reduce the risk of certain diseases, such as type 2 diabetes, liver disease, certain cancers, and Alzheimer's dementia, and thus has preventive health benefits. In any case, based on the current data situation, scientists believe that reducing coffee consumption as a general health recommendation for the population and patients is not necessary.
