Well, I was supposed to wake up in the morning and leave at 9:40 to catch the saland (water boat) with my bike, but I delayed it by 1 hour with the intention of going a little slower. When we go out, our man runs at full speed as if he is in a hurry, haha. Well, it's true that you have an iBike, but your iBike also has a speed limit. If the speed increases from 25 km/h, the motor stops supporting it. Well, I was driving with my friend at 30 km/h[эргийн боомт тосгон Дагебюл дээр ирлээ](https://www.gemeinde-dagebuell.de/).

The road between Niibyul and Dagebyul 2 is really cool. The car was parked a little lower right next to the road, so it was mainly passing by the wheat fields... and most of all, the salty air of the sea, I liked it very much as a Mongolian. I mostly felt like riding a horse across the beautiful Mongolian plains.

Haha, I drove right to the port and met them loading travelers to the raft. I put my bike against the wall on the floor of the car and took a position as if I was standing in the front zero parking lot on the big open field above. When people are seen, they keep saying that they are covering up. After 20-30 minutes, the captain of the plane skillfully maneuvered through the water reduced by the sea rope and arrived at Fayor Island.
