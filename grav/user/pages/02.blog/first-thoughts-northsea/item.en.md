Welcome to my first blog post!
Today, August 10, 2021, I traveled about 1000 km with my friend by express and regular train. After settling down at my vacation home, I drank some red wine that was given to me by an acquaintance to drink on my vacation, then I drank a new private brand rum made from rum aged 21 years in a wooden barrel that used to hold bourbon whiskey.

On Sergeant's Saturday, I got the Webserver ready and installed on the Raspberry Pi, and before I got up on Sunday to cook and go back to bed, I installed this blog's original GravSiMS.

Right now, I was a bit confused about how to enter structured information on any topic, but I couldn't bear the rest of my tired body from the long journey, so I closed my eyes...
