What happened to the game? It seems that people are very sensitive when it comes to games, and it seems that people who play games have a bad reputation, 😝. Yes, I am a very bad person then, 🤓. In general, games played on smartphones and computers (PC = personal computer) are called Gaming...

===

Let's stop calling it.

On the other hand, I like to wrestle and struggle with some technique, programming, and some subject, so I call all the issues related to them as playing my games and actions.

Looking at most people, Gaming has taught them a bad lesson in life. It seems that he is angry to talk about games and play. Yes, I'm sorry, it's your fault for bothering yourself,🤭. I've probably had one or two similar problems, so I'm not interested in throwing this thread away. Because it has already become an integral part of my life. And if you are not interested in understanding and accepting me, it is pointless to be friends with me, 😜.

There are people who are angry to play on PC, but if they have time on their mobile phones, they play all day, all year, ten years. I don't play it because I don't feel like playing it on mobile. Besides, I wonder how busy I am, 😅🤣. Every day, every month, every few months, every year, if there is no new game, I just play it for 30 minutes, ok, sometimes 1 hour. Family, work and life are the 1st, but for that I have to be healthy. In addition to recreation, sports and friends, its positive dimension includes Gaming.

I basically don't play on my phone. But on the PC, after playing all the games, at least the solo part is completed in about 1 day or 24 hours with normal difficulty (mode = hard) at most. And then, if you like it, you finish playing at the most difficult stage (pro =) and connect to the Internet to play together with other people.
Space Invaders is the first game released on almost all PCs. Open data is not collected. Just played, finished, satisfied, that's it,😊😇.

So, if I say that I am playing on my mobile phone, then I am not gaming by nature, so I am just doing all kinds of technical and usability tests that I am interested in,😉.

On the other hand, if you think about it, little kids get into Gaming and don't know what to do anymore. Also, the bad things associated with money that usually have a bad name like gambling, have given a bad name to the good pleasure of **GAMING**, yes, 🤓😎.

For my kids, I only let them play Minecraft and age-appropriate games. I spent a lot of time meeting friends, playing outside and going out in the wind. The internet is automatically turned off after midnight except for my work PC. A good router has a function that can limit children's time, I used it when I was young. In addition, time and other limits have been set on the children's PC with their MicroSoft Account. In general, there is a lot of fighting to limit pornography and other inappropriate content. But when he reached 16, he entered real Gaming. After about 1 year of occasional play, he has stopped playing and instead hangs out with me, shoots food, bakes cakes, watches movies, etc. I had a lot of fun dates, 😌😍🥰. Grow up, baby, 🥳.
