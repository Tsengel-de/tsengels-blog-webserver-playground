Today, I have done the translation of the back of this used Sims.
It doesn't matter if there's a wrong translation, it's not about waiting or pretending to be great. Anyway, started it and dropped it. If I can't make it, maybe someone will like it and improve it, 😁.

Of course, I think the meaning will become clearer during use.

Here's a quick note on how to improve this blog or the backend system:

1. New installation of Grav CMS blog system by combining translated backend (Core Grav CMS) and administration (Grav Admin)
2. To improve the translation so that the user can understand it
3. Make it multilingual, Mongolian, English and German are considered sufficient in the first place
4. Checking and commissioning useful extensions in the control panel
5. Move the webserver to Raspi4 (Raspberry Pie 4).
6. Entering records by topic
7. To improve the structure of the page and the organization of records

First of all, I thought about what I would do if my passion and hobby moved, and I uploaded it here...

Well, the owner of the vacation home or a friend's cousin also wanted to make a brief picture of the vacation in German and English, so that's the main purpose of deciding to do it in multiple languages, 😇😎.
If the place is free, I will do it now, I won't take money 😅😁
