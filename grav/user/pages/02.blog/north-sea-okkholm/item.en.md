Today it is in a village called Okholm, 18-20 km away[Steak House-д](https://www.dat-luette.de/)It is planned to eat food (meat). At night, for some reason, I woke up and felt like I didn't sleep enough. I got up at 12 o'clock and when I left the cafe to cook and drink, I didn't notice that it was already 2 o'clock.
I eat dinner early and I don't eat before 12 o'clock on the day, so when I looked at the clock, it was 16 o'clock. Since we had an appointment at Steak House at 5 p.m., we were able to ride our bikes freely, but there was a very strong wind from the front. The North Sea is known for its stormy winds.
I eat meat at 12-2pm, I got so angry that I shut up, haha.
It wasn't time yet, so I lay down in the grass on the embankment on the other side of the sea and sunbathe for a while.
The force of the wind and the rapid movement of the clouds brought crocodile tears to my hungry eyes that suddenly reminded me of home.
On the side of the road, a car drove by, but for some reason it felt like driving against the wind in an unoccupied field, the streets were empty...
