Today, for the first time, let's share our experience on how to improve our language skills. I like to say that it is easy to learn other languages ​​when one has learned one language well! Why is it easy to learn other languages ​​when you master one language?

===

- In order to improve your knowledge of the language of the country where you were born and grew up, summarize the way you went through school and think about it, adding daily use. Hmm, that's it, 😉. Having your own way of learning, disciplined and principled, will be your fastest way to success and will save you time as well. In addition to learning, I want everyone to have fun when they are children and youth, 😎🤩🥳.

Those who attend school carefully and learn to do their homework on time will easily overcome other problems in life and have enough free time. It is important to know that questions only grow as you begin to learn and understand.

_**Put it into one continuous cycle of planning, testing, doing, improving, and then there is a very high chance that it will go well**_

#### So, how I used the method I used to learn Mongolian to learn a foreign language:
1. Find a good grammar book
2. Find a book with good grammar review answers
3. A good dictionary, in the same language (For example: Mongolian is Mongolian, English is English language dictionary)
4. Watch the news for 30 minutes every evening and understand the words you don't know by reading the explanation from the dictionary on the spot
5. In the first year, he should order and read a newspaper that is said to be the best in the country and is written at the highest level of professional language (the biggest newspaper?!).
6. Write information about your country in the language you are learning/improving (abstract) = Information about the whole country is a huge amount of information, and you can compare it on Wikipedia and plan how to write it first.
7. Make friends and hang out and learn and practice slanging = where your essay came from in the first place, sparking initial conversation and allowing you to continue talking very quickly.
8. 5-10 minutes a day only to repeat the rules, of course with exercises. The reason why it is a book with optional answers is that it is easy to check yourself, not to copy.
9. Find an encyclopedia in your language, and if you have time, look into the fields you don't know or are interested in.
10. Read literary books in your own language
11. Explain everything from the dictionary. At first, it may seem time-consuming, but with experience, you are well prepared to give professional explanations even on scientific issues.

_**If you want to save time, buy and order, if you want to save money, go to the library regularly.**_ If you study with the above methods, you will have the opportunity to reach B2 of the international standard level in half a year. It is said that this is the minimum level of language required to become a citizen of that country (probably the most important thing is that it is enough to work). For example, in Germany...
