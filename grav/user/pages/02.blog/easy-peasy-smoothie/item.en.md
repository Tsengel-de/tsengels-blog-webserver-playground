The easiest way to make a smoothie is this. There are many types of special mixers (Mixer, Smoothie Maker). If you buy a nice one for yourself, you will use it for a long time.

===

**The recipe is:**
!!! * 1 banana
!!! * 1 apple
!!! * 50-80 g of frozen fruit (varieties of anise and rock)
!!! * 50-100 ml of apple juice or cold water
!!!
!!! Entry for 2 people

I've used almost all of them, and right now I'm using the Hand Blender, because it cleans and harvests without using a battery, so it's easy. Such a smoothie can be made 1-2 times a week.

Just cut it into small pieces and put it in the mixer. One of the benefits of frozen fruit is that you don't have to manually or separately mix the ice
